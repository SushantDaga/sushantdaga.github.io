I build machine-learning systems for work where being wrong has consequences. I founded [Biclay](https://biclay.com){:rel="noopener"}, and I am building [CiteOnly](https://citeonly.com){:rel="noopener"} there. CiteOnly cannot make things up. It generates only citations, word for word from your own documents, each with its source beside it. Free generation is where fabrication happens; other systems generate freely and add checks afterwards. CiteOnly has no free-generation step.

I have been interested in machine learning since college, and have worked on it since:  NLP inference and generation at VMock, core ML for document understanding at NanoNets, and applied-ML consulting at Biclay Labs. I studied **Electrical Engineering** at **IIT Delhi**.

I am interested in using technology to uplift people and raise the standard of living. Away from work I dance, cook, and travel, and I am a foodie. I am a sucker for new experiences and for learning and trying new things.
